# Highlights

1. ECNetBench provides a leakage-safe, multi-seed enterprise cognitive networking benchmark with frozen SQLite instances and published checksums.
2. An Enterprise Cognitive Network couples a Digital Twin with Perception, Anomaly, Prediction, RCA, Impact, and Healing agents under anchored fusion.
3. On six seeds, proposed T1 AUPRC mean is 0.05771284608153401 versus random forest 0.07583814878524626; T2 proposed mean is 0.0381064340665632, tied with logistic 0.038043099063649714.
4. Ablations show telemetry as the dominant contributor; Digital Twin effects are task-dependent (negative on T1, modest positive on T2).
5. The primary contribution is a complete, reproducible detection→prediction→RCA→impact→healing decision-support pipeline with honest statistics—not universal detector superiority.
