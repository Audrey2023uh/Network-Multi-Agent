# Journal Fit Report

## Recommended primary target
**IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management (TNSM)**

Rationale: network/service management scope; AIOps and automation; empirical multi-seed evaluation; management-plane contributions (RCA/healing decision support) fit TNSM better than pure theory venues.

## Strong alternatives
1. **Journal of Network and Systems Management** — management systems and operational tooling.
2. **Computer Networks** — broader networking systems + evaluation.
3. **IEEE Network** — if shortened to a magazine-style systems perspective (would require rewriting length/style).

## Fit strengths
- Benchmark + framework dual contribution
- Leakage-safe protocol and statistical honesty
- Enterprise/AOS-CX grounding

## Fit risks
- Synthetic-only data (external validity)
- Non-superiority vs RF on T1 may draw reviewer pushback unless framing emphasizes system completeness (already addressed)
- Healing macro-F1=1.0 with RCA categories must remain clearly caveated (already addressed)

## Recommended positioning sentence for cover letter
“We contribute a reproducible enterprise cognitive-networking benchmark and a twin/multi-agent management pipeline evaluated with leakage-safe multi-seed statistics, without claiming universal superiority over strong tabular detectors.”
